About Alder & Cove

Showroom calm, artisan warmth.

Alder & Cove was shaped around a simple idea: bath furnishings should feel composed. Not overstyled. Not cluttered. Not disposable. Just well presented, well chosen pieces with enough material presence to anchor a room and enough practicality to work every day.

Our visual language leans clean and architectural, but never cold. Stone, walnut, charcoal, soft white, and restrained brass are the cues behind the storefront. The result is a quieter shopping experience built to feel more like a design studio than a marketplace.
