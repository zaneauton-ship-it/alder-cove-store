Shipping policy

Shipping & delivery

This Shipping Policy explains how Alder & Cove, operated by Foothills Online Listings LLC, processes and ships orders placed through this site.

Processing time

Orders are typically processed within 72 hours of successful payment confirmation, excluding weekends and holidays.

Shipping scope

At launch, checkout is configured for orders shipping within the United States. We may expand to additional regions later.

Order confirmation

Once your order is placed, you will receive an order confirmation through Stripe or the email address used during checkout. If shipment tracking becomes available, it will be provided to you by email.

Delivery timing

Transit times vary by product, destination, and carrier. Processing time is separate from shipping transit time.

Incorrect addresses

Please confirm your shipping details before completing checkout. We are not responsible for delays or failed delivery caused by inaccurate address information supplied at checkout.

Shipping issues

If your order arrives visibly damaged or incomplete, contact info@foothillslistings.online as soon as possible and include your order number, photos, and a short description of the issue.
