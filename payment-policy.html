Payment policy

Payment Policy

Payments on Alder & Cove are processed through embedded Stripe Checkout.

Accepted payment processing

Available payment methods are determined by your Stripe account configuration and the methods enabled in your Stripe Dashboard.

Authorization

Orders may be subject to payment authorization, verification, and anti-fraud review before processing begins.

Taxes

Applicable taxes are calculated during checkout based on the order information and the tax configuration in Stripe.

Charge disputes

If you believe a charge was made in error, please contact info@foothillslistings.online before initiating a dispute so we can review the issue and attempt to resolve it directly.

Refunds

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method used at checkout.
