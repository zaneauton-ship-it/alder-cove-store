Returns & refunds

30-day return policy

Alder & Cove accepts returns for eligible items within 30 days of delivery.

Eligibility

  • Items must be returned within 30 days of delivery.
  • Items should be unused and in materially the same condition in which they were received.
  • Items should include original packaging where reasonably possible.

How to start a return

Email info@foothillslistings.online with your order number, reason for return, and any relevant photos if the item is damaged or defective.

Damaged or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or not as described, contact us promptly so we can review the issue and determine the most appropriate resolution.

Refund timing

Approved refunds are issued back to the original payment method. Timing can vary depending on your financial institution.

Non-returnable situations

We may decline returns where an item shows signs of use, has been altered after delivery, or is returned outside the stated return window unless otherwise required by law.
