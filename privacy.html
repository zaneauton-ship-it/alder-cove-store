Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Alder & Cove, operated by Foothills Online Listings LLC, collects, uses, and protects information when you visit this website or place an order.

Information we collect

  • Contact and checkout information you provide, such as name, email, billing address, and shipping address.
  • Order details and product selections.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device information, and usage data collected through standard website analytics or server logs.

How we use your information

  • To process and fulfill orders.
  • To communicate about purchases, returns, and customer service requests.
  • To maintain, improve, and secure the website.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

Payments

Payments are processed through Stripe. We do not store full payment card information on our servers. Please review Stripe's privacy terms and related policies for additional information about payment processing.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers as necessary to operate the site, process payments, calculate taxes, or fulfill and support orders.

Your choices

You may contact us at info@foothillslistings.online regarding privacy questions or requests related to the information you have provided.

Policy updates
